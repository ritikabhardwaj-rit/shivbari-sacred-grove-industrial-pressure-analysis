# shivbari-sacred-grove-industrial-pressure-analysis
GIS-based assessment of industrial pressure on the 26 ha Shiv Bari Sacred Grove, Himachal Pradesh. Analysis includes sacred grove boundary mapping, 500 m and 1000 m buffer zones, industry inventory, and spatial proximity assessment using ArcGIS Pro and Google Earth Pro.
This map shows:

- Geographic location of Shiv Bari Sacred Grove within Una District, Himachal Pradesh.
- Sacred grove boundary.
- Nearby industrial establishments.
- Shiv Bari Temple location.
- Road network and settlement (Ambota).
- Spatial relationship between the sacred grove and surrounding industrial clusters.

The map serves as a baseline visualization for assessing potential industrial pressure on the sacred grove ecosystem.
