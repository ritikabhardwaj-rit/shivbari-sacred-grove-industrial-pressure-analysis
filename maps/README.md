# Maps

This folder contains all GIS maps produced during the Shiv Bari Sacred Grove Industrial Pressure Assessment.

## Contents

- Sacred Grove Boundary Map
- 500 m Buffer Map
- 1000 m Buffer Map
- Industrial Pressure Map
- Distance Analysis Maps

## Key Findings

- Sacred Grove Area: 26 ha
- Industries within 500 m buffer: 5
- Industries within 1000 m buffer: 16
- Nearest industry distance: 212.36 m

## Analysis Summary

Spatial analysis indicates that industrial activities are concentrated in the vicinity of Shiv Bari Sacred Grove. A total of 5 industries were identified within a 500 m buffer and 16 industries within a 1000 m buffer. The nearest recorded industry is located approximately 212.36 m from the sacred grove boundary, indicating potential industrial influence on the grove's ecological integrity.
